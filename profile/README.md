# DisclosureWorks

**Putting evidence to work in the public interest.**

DisclosureWorks is an independent research initiative. We analyze disclosed and public records (government documents, congressional records and court files) and publish what they show, with every claim tied to the document that supports it. Our current focus is the oversight of national security power.

## What we publish

- **[Sourcebook](https://sourcebook.disclosureworks.ai)**: short, cited entries on the people, programs, laws and events in the record, published in fixed monthly editions. Source repository: [sourcebook](https://github.com/DisclosureWorks/sourcebook).
- **[Publications](https://publications.disclosureworks.ai)**: research papers and legal memos built from primary sources. Source repository: [publications](https://github.com/DisclosureWorks/publications).

Our work is produced with Parallax, our research system, and approved by people before it is published. Parallax is not yet available outside DisclosureWorks.
